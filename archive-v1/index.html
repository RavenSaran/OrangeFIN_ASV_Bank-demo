Automated Signature Verification

Stop forged signatures before the money moves.

ASV reads the signature on a scanned bank instruction, compares it with the verified specimen of the right authorised signatory, and routes anything doubtful to a human reviewer.

The shared pipeline

Four steps, one human decision

01 Receive

Scan the signed form

Payment form, FD upliftment or transfer instruction.

02 Extract

Crop the signature

Overlap or poor capture is flagged, not guessed.

03 Compare

Match to the specimen

Against the signatory required by the mandate.

04 Route

Staff decides

Approve, recheck manually or reject. Every action is logged.

ASV supports the bank. It does not replace it.

A low score does not prove forgery and a high score does not authorise a payment. ASV flags what needs a closer look so staff spend their time on the cases that matter.
